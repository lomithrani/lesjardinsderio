Events & celebrations

A talent for making moments unforgettable

Les Jardins de Rio offers large, medium and small adaptable spaces for your event. For all of them, the house can be privatized from noon onwards.

Types of events

  • Weddings, bridal parties, birthdays
  • Lunches, cocktails and dinners
  • Cultural festivals, exhibitions, cinema, music, gastronomy
  • Business and academic conferences
  • Seminars, workshops, talks
  • Film shoots, TV, photography, fashion shows, media

We can also provide

  • Catering, sweet and savoury buffets
  • Barman, open bar, cocktails, wine selections
  • Use of the pool
  • Music, DJs, live bands, video projections and art installations
  • Personalized decoration
  • Security, cleaning staff and valet parking

Full privatization

All 8 suites and every space of the house, just for you and your guests. Ideal for destination weddings, retreats and productions.
